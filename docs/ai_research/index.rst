Responsible and efficient use of AI for research
================================================

.. rst-class:: lead

    AI is an **enabler** — it extends what you can attempt, and helps you deepen
    your own expertise rather than replace it. Generating a first version of code
    or text is now the easy part; the judgment to design it well, and to know what
    you actually want, is your responsibility. Picture the outcome, state it
    clearly, and today's models are good at turning that intent into a first draft.

A first draft is not a finished result, though. These tools are fluent and fast,
but confidently wrong often enough that you have to check what they produce — and
whatever you keep is yours to stand behind. This theme has two aims — use AI to
make your research **better**, and use it **responsibly** — and they come down to
the same habit: aim high, then own and check the result.
